Warehouse

Warehouse

Facilities

Go to Warehouse → Facilities to manage CFS (Container Freight Station) and warehouse locations.

  • Create facilities with name, location code, address, total area (sqm), and contact details
  • Mark facilities as Bonded or approved for Dangerous Goods
  • Track reefer capacity for temperature-controlled cargo

CFS Inventory

Go to Warehouse → Inventory and select a facility to view all cargo items currently in that location. Each item shows its condition on receipt:

Good Damaged Short Excess Wet Contaminated

Inventory records link back to the originating shipment, making it easy to raise claims or exceptions directly from the warehouse view.
