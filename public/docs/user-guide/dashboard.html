Dashboard

Dashboard

The Dashboard is your real-time operational overview showing where your business stands today.

Stat Cards

Seven stat cards provide an instant summary of your shipment portfolio:

  • Active — shipments currently in progress
  • Pending — shipments awaiting confirmation or action
  • Confirmed — booked and confirmed jobs
  • Arriving (next 7 days) — shipments with ETA within one week
  • Overdue — shipments past their expected delivery date
  • Exceptions — shipments flagged with issues or delays
  • Done (this month) — shipments completed in the current calendar month
Tip: Clicking any stat card navigates directly to a filtered shipment list showing only the shipments matching that status.

Monthly Trend Chart

A bar chart displays shipment volume month by month, giving a clear visual of growth trends or seasonal dips. Hover over a bar to see the exact count for that month.

Upcoming Arrivals

A table lists all shipments with an ETA in the next 7 days, helping operations staff plan for incoming cargo.
