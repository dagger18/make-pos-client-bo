Library

Library

Master reference data used across the system — set up once, available everywhere. Keeping Library data accurate ensures quotes, shipments, and financial documents are consistent and complete.

Charges

  • Freight Charges — base charge definitions by transport mode
  • Local Charges — port and documentation fees
  • Customs Charges — clearance and duty-related charges
  • Service Charges — insurance, fumigation, labelling, etc.

Trade & Customs

  • HS Codes — harmonized tariff codes for commodity classification
  • HS Restrictions — country-specific import/export restrictions
  • Duty Rates — duty rates linked to HS codes by country
  • Incoterms — FOB, CIF, DDP, EXW, and all standard trade terms

Tax

  • Tax Groups — VAT/GST groupings by country
  • Tax Rules — zero-rated, exempt, and reverse charge rules
  • Tax Exemptions — client-specific tax exemptions
  • Tax Registrations — company tax IDs by country

Operations

  • Package Types — container, pallet, carton, and other unit definitions
  • Shipment Modes — FCL, LCL, FTL, LTL, Charter, and more
  • Free Time Agreements — port free day policies by carrier and port
  • Carrier Event Mapping — standardise carrier status codes to system statuses

Finance & Insurance

  • Payment Methods — bank transfer, card, cheque, and other methods
  • Insurance Policies — cargo insurance contract details
  • Cargo Claims — insurance claim management and tracking

Compliance

  • Sanctions Lists — restricted parties and sanctioned countries
  • Emission Factors — CO₂ coefficients by transport mode for emissions calculations
  • Data Retention — archive and purge policies for records management
