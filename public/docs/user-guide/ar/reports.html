التقارير

رؤى تشغيلية ومالية وامتثالية عبر جميع أنحاء المؤسسة. تتطلب معظم التقارير نطاقاً زمنياً — حدد تاريخَي البدء والانتهاء وانقر تشغيل التقرير.

تقارير العمليات

ReportWhat it shows
ShipmentAll shipments with status, client, carrier, route, and financial summary. Filterable by mode, date range, and status.
ExceptionShipments flagged as exceptions — delayed, missing docs, escalated issues — with reason codes and current responsible party.
StaffWorkload and productivity metrics per staff member, including shipments handled and tasks completed.

التقارير المالية

ReportWhat it shows
ChargeDetailed charge breakdown by type, shipment, and currency across the selected period.
VAT ReportOutput tax, input tax, net VAT payable, and withholding tax for the period. Export for tax filings.
Insurance DeclarationDeclared cargo values and insurance premiums for all insured shipments in the period.

التحليلات ومؤشرات الأداء

ReportWhat it shows
KPI DashboardOn-time delivery rate, DSO (days sales outstanding), quote conversion rate by month, operator productivity.
Customer ProfitabilityRevenue, cost, gross profit, and margin % per client for the period.
Department P&LProfit & loss broken down by branch or department.
Top LanesBusiest trade lanes by shipment count and revenue.
Rate BenchmarkBuy vs. market, sell vs. market, and market trend charts for evaluating rate competitiveness.
Carrier PerformanceOn-time and reliability scores by carrier across all your shipments.
Sales CommissionCommission earned per sales staff member based on closed shipments in the period.
Sales TargetRevenue target vs. actual by staff member or branch for the period.
CO₂ EmissionsEstimated and actual carbon emissions by shipment and transport mode. Export for sustainability reporting.

تقارير الامتثال

ReportWhat it shows
Compliance DashboardOverview of sanctions screenings, restricted cargo alerts, and compliance flags across active shipments.
Audit LogComplete immutable record of all system actions — who, what, when, and result (Success / Failure / Blocked). Filter by event type, actor, and date.
EDI LogHistory of all electronic data interchange messages sent to and received from carriers and partners.
D&D DashboardDemurrage & Detention status across all active shipments — containers in dispute, returned, or actively accruing fees.
