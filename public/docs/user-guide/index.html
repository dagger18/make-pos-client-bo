Overview

Overview

Make Cargo is an end-to-end freight forwarding back-office platform covering pricing, quoting, shipment execution, financial management, and compliance reporting.

Transport Modes

  • Ocean — FCL & LCL
  • Air
  • Road — FTL & LTL
  • Rail
  • Courier
  • Multimodal

Typical Workflow

Rate Setup

Shipment
Invoice
Payment

Jump to a module

  • Dashboard — Real-time operational overview
  • Quotes — Create and manage shipping quotations
  • Shipments — Core freight job management
  • Consolidations — Group LCL shipments into containers
  • Clients — Customer profiles and billing history
  • Providers — Carrier and vendor management
  • Accounting — Invoices, payments, and financial documents
  • Rates — Buying and selling price lists
  • Carrier Tools — Schedules and container tracking
  • Warehouse — CFS and inventory management
  • CRM — Sales pipeline and leads
  • Reports — Operational, financial, and compliance reports
  • Library — Master reference data
  • Settings — Users, branches, integrations

Logging In

  1. 1Go to your company's Make Cargo URL (e.g., app.makecargo.io)
  2. 2Enter your username (email) and password
  3. 3Click Login. You will be taken to the Dashboard.

Forgot Password? Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page and enter your email address. A password reset link will be sent to your inbox.

Invitation Link: New users receive an invitation email with a link to set their password. The link expires after 48 hours — contact your administrator if it has expired.

Navigation

The left sidebar provides access to all modules. Sub-tabs become visible after opening a module. The top-right corner shows your profile avatar, notification bell, and language switcher. A red badge ● appears on items that require attention or have new activity.

Language & RTL Support

Make Cargo supports multiple languages. Switch your language at any time using the language switcher in the top-right corner of the application.

English 中文 (Simplified) Tiếng Việt 日本語 한국어 Deutsch Español العربية (RTL)

When Arabic is selected, the entire interface switches to right-to-left (RTL) layout — sidebars, text alignment, and flow are all mirrored automatically.

Your language preference is saved to your profile and persists across sessions and devices.
