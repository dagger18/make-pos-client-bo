Accounting

The Accounting module handles all financial documents — invoices, credit notes, payments, and receipts.

Document Types

CodeNamePurpose
IDDebit Note / Sales InvoiceCharge a client for freight services
ICCredit Note / Cost InvoiceRecord cost from a provider
COCOBOInternal cost-to-owner document
POPOBO / Purchase OrderPurchase order / payable record
PMTPaymentRecord a payment made to a provider
RPTReceiptRecord a payment received from a client

Creating an Invoice (ID)

  1. 1Go to Accounting → ID and click New Invoice, or create directly from the shipment Cost Sheet tab
  2. 2Select the client and shipment reference
  3. 3Add charge lines (imported from cost sheet or entered manually)
  4. 4Set the currency and exchange rate
  5. 5Save as Draft, then click Submit to finalise and issue to the client

Statement of Account

Navigate to Accounting → SOA-Client or open a client/provider record and select Statement of Account. The SOA shows:

  • All invoices and their current status (Draft / Submitted / Approved / Paid)
  • All payments and the invoices they were applied to
  • Running outstanding balance
  • Multi-currency display with editable exchange rates

Export the SOA as PDF or Excel for client review or internal reconciliation.

Recording Payments & Receipts

Go to Accounting → PMT to record a payment made to a provider, or Accounting → RPT to record a payment received from a client. In each case:

  1. Create a new record and link it to the relevant invoice(s)
  2. Enter the payment amount, date, and payment method (bank transfer, card, cheque, etc.)
  3. Save — the outstanding balance on the linked invoice(s) updates automatically

Ageing Reports

Go to Accounting → AR Ageing (receivables) or AP Ageing (payables). Select a date range and run the report. Outstanding amounts are grouped by age band:

Current 1–30 days 31–60 days 61–90 days 90+ days

Journal Entries

Go to Accounting → Journal to create manual general ledger entries. Each journal entry requires:

  • Debit and credit account codes from the Chart of Accounts
  • Description and reference
  • Amount and currency

Journal entries require approval from an authorised user before they are posted to the general ledger.

P&L by Period

Go to Accounting → P&L Period. Select a from/to date range and click Run Report. The report shows revenue, cost, gross profit, margin %, FX gain/loss, and net profit broken down by branch.
